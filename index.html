How we work

Replicas designed to be built like a real object, not to keep you at the workbench for weeks.

  • Real screws

    Most parts join with real metric screws instead of glue. They hold firm, and you can take them apart whenever you like.

  • Removable parts

    Removable display magazines and parts you can take out and put back. Made to be handled and displayed, not just looked at behind glass.

  • No sanding, no painting

    It arrives with its final finish: the color comes from the filament you choose. No filler, no sandpaper, no airbrush. Unbox, build, display.

  • Make it yours, if you want

    Into the battle-worn look? The parts take paint and weathering with no trouble: add wear, rust or trench mud to taste.

From the forge to your shelf

  1. Your colors

    Choose the color of the key parts from the filament we have on the shelf right now.

  2. Printed for you

    We start printing when your payment arrives. Ideal shipping time: about 3 weeks from then.

  3. Built by you

    It arrives disassembled, like flat-pack furniture. Put it together in an evening.
